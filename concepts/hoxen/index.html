What is inside 104 grams

Scroll to open

  1. 58 °Cat the surface, at full load

    Shell and heat spreader

    A flame-rated polycarbonate shell with 1.8 mm walls. The aluminium plate behind it carries heat away from the board, so the outside stays cool enough to hold.

  2. 93.2%efficient at 65 W

    Power stage

    A copper-wound transformer and three capacitors turn mains voltage into what your devices take. Less than seven per cent of it is lost as heat.

  3. 45 + 20 Wto two devices at once

    Port board

    Gallium-nitride switches do the work silicon needed three times the space for. Plug in a second device and the board re-shares the power in under a second.

  4. 10,000insertions per port

    Front plate

    Each port has a glass-filled nylon tongue, moulded in orange so you can find it in a dark room. The plate is welded, not glued.
