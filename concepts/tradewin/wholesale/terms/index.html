Supplier terms

How Tradewin Wholesale trades, in nine lines. No account manager is needed to read them.

  1. Minimum

    One carton of one line

    Cartons are sealed at the workshop and are not split. Mix as many lines as you like on an order.

  2. Prices

    Step down at 5 and 25 cartons

    Counted per line, per order. The catalogue and the order sheet both show the price you have reached.

  3. Payment

    On dispatch

    Card or bank transfer, taken when the order leaves Yiwu, three working days after you send it.

  4. Air freight

    $4.80 a kilo, 6 to 9 days

    Door to door, duties paid, to any of the 41 countries we ship to.

  5. Sea freight

    $0.90 a kilo, 32 to 38 days

    Consolidated weekly from Ningbo. Delivered duty paid to your door, not to the port.

  6. Samples

    One of each, at the Direct price

    Order singles from Tradewin Direct. We credit them against your first carton of that line.

  7. Your label

    From 25 cartons of a line

    Your logo on the pack insert and the carton, at no charge, with ten working days added.

  8. Claims

    60 days from delivery

    Photograph the fault and the carton label. Faulty units are credited at what you paid, without being sent back.

  9. Who you are buying from

    Yiwu Tradewin Global Supply Chain Co., Ltd.

    Building 7, 1188 Chouzhou North Road, Yiwu 322000, Zhejiang, China. Trading since 2014.
