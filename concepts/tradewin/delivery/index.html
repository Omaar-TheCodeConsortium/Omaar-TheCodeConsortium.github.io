Delivery, duties and returns

Seven things people ask before they buy from a shop 8,900 km away, answered with the numbers.

    $3.90 on any order

    Free once the goods come to $35. The basket shows how far off you are.

    7 to 12 days

    Door to door. Orders in by 4 pm Yiwu time are packed the same day and fly from Hangzhou within three.

  3. Tracking

    Five stops, all scanned

    Your number starts working when the parcel leaves the warehouse, usually the day after you order.

    Paid by us

    We settle import duty and sales tax before the parcel leaves China. The courier asks you for nothing.

  5. More than one

    10% off two, 15% off three

    Of the same product, taken off each one. It costs us less to pack them together.

    30 days, our label

    Send anything back unused within 30 days of delivery. We email a prepaid label and refund when it is scanned.

    Refund on day 21

    If tracking has not moved for seven days, or the parcel has not arrived 21 days after packing, we refund or resend. Your choice.
