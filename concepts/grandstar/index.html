Thirty working days from order to port

Follow one part through the building. This is a typical programme for a single-cavity P20 tool and a first order of 5,000 parts. Hardened tools add five days. Tools with four or more cavities add four.

  1. Controller housing, PC/ABS. Third-angle projection, dimensions in millimetres.

    Day00–05

    On the drawing

    Before any steel is ordered, the engineer who will own your project reports on draft, wall thickness, gate position and ejection. Nothing is cut until you have signed it.

    • Day 0 to 2DFM reviewDraft, wall, gate and ejection report returned for sign-off
    • Day 2 to 5Tool design3D tool design and steel order

  2. Day05–14

    In the tool room

    Forty-one toolmakers cut, harden and fit the mould forty metres from the press it will run on. Cavity and core steel is held to ±0.02 mm.

    • Day 5 to 12MachiningCNC roughing, heat treatment where specified, finishing, EDM
    • Day 12 to 14Fit and polishBench fitting, spotting, surface finish

  3. Day14–23

    First shots

    The first parts off the tool are measured against every dimension on your drawing and flown to you. Five of these days are yours: the samples are in the air and on your engineers’ desks.

    • Day 14 to 15T1 trialFirst shots, dimensional report, samples shipped by air
    • Day 15 to 20Your approvalSamples in transit and with your engineers
    • Day 20 to 23CorrectionsT2 if dimensions or cosmetics need adjusting

  4. Day23–29

    Production, measured

    The order is moulded, checked at the press and packed. What we measure travels with the parts, as a report you can read without us.

    • Day 23 to 29First productionMoulding, in-process checks, packing

  5. Day29–30

    To the port

    Cartons are checked against your purchase order and trucked to Yantian, ninety minutes away. From here the lead time is the ship’s.

    • Day 29 to 30FOB ShenzhenOutgoing inspection and delivery to Yantian port
