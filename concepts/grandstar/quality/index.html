The four gates

  1. 1

    Gate 1: Incoming

    IQC

    • Resin lot matched to its certificate of analysis
    • Melt flow index verified on one bag per lot
    • Tool steel hardness and certificate checked before cutting

  2. 2

    Gate 2: First article

    FAI

    • Every drawing dimension measured on five shots per cavity
    • Report issued with balloon drawing before production is released
    • Colour compared to the signed master under D65 light

  3. 3

    Gate 3: In process

    IPQC

    • Critical dimensions every two hours, charted for Cpk
    • Part weight logged every hour as an early drift signal
    • Cosmetic check against boundary samples at the press

  4. 4

    Gate 4: Outgoing

    OQC

    • AQL 0.65 major and 1.5 minor to ISO 2859-1 unless you specify otherwise
    • Carton drop test on the first shipment of each part
    • Labels and quantities checked against your purchase order

Ownership, payment and shipping

The questions buyers ask before placing a first order with a supplier they have not met.

Price a part

Who owns the mould?

You do, once the tooling invoice is paid in full. Ownership is written into the tooling agreement, the mould is engraved with your asset number, and it is never used for another customer. We store and maintain it at no charge while it is in production with us.

Can I move my mould to another factory?

Yes. Give us ten working days and we will clean, rust-proof and crate the tool, and hand over the 2D and 3D tool drawings, the steel certificates and the last-shot samples. You pay the crating and freight.

How do you protect our design files?

We sign your NDA or our mutual one before any file is opened. CAD is held on an internal server with access per project, tool-room terminals have no USB ports or internet access, and we do not photograph customer parts for marketing without written permission.

What are the payment terms?

Tooling is 50% with the order and 50% on approval of T1 samples. Production orders are 30% deposit and 70% before shipment. After three production orders, customers with a clean record can apply for 30-day terms.

What is the minimum order quantity?

For moulded parts, 500 pieces per order per colour, because material changeover and machine setup cost the same for 50 parts as for 5,000. For 3D printed parts there is no minimum.

Which shipping terms do you offer?

Our prices are FOB Shenzhen (Yantian). We also quote EXW, CIF and DAP on request. Air freight to Europe or North America typically takes 5 to 8 days door to door, and sea freight 28 to 38 days depending on the port.

What happens if parts arrive out of specification?

Send photos and measurements within 30 days of receipt. We reply within one working day with a containment plan, and replace or credit confirmed non-conforming parts at our cost, including air freight for the replacement lot. Every claim gets an 8D report.
