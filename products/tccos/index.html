TCC OS

The people system for companies that run around the clock.

TCC OS runs the staff side of a BPO or any large shift-based business: who works where and when, leave and attendance, payroll, and the vans that bring people in for the night shift.

See an early build

What it does

  • People recordsStatusEvery employee, their documents and their place in the organisation.
  • RostersStatusShift patterns, rotations and the master rota.
  • Leave and attendanceStatusApplications, approvals and biometric clock-ins.
  • PayrollStatusSalaries, EPF and ETF, and statutory holidays.
  • Staff transportStatusRoutes and manifests for the vans that bring shifts in.
  • Organisation chartStatusRestructure teams by dragging the lines between people.
