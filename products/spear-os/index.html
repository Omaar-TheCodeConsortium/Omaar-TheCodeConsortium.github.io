Spear OS

One system for running a hotel and its restaurant.

Spear OS runs the rooms, the restaurant, the kitchen, the guests and the staff of a hotel in one system. Bookings from every channel land on one calendar, dinner is charged straight to the room, and a stay and everything charged to it end up on one bill.

Hotel5 modules

  • 01Hotel PMSBuiltRooms, reservations and the guest folio in one record.
  • 02Booking engineBuiltCommission-free bookings on the hotel’s own website.
  • 03Channel managerBuiltBooking.com, Expedia, Agoda and Airbnb kept in step, so no room is sold twice.
  • 04Revenue managementBuiltRates that follow occupancy and demand, sent to every channel at once.
  • 05Sales and banquetingBuiltEvent orders that block rooms, hold spaces and order the food.

Restaurant7 modules

  • 06Offline POSBuiltA desktop till that keeps trading when the internet drops.
  • 07QR and tablet orderingBuiltGuests order from the table, waiters from a tablet.
  • 08Menus and recipesBuiltEvery dish costed to the gram.
  • 09Kitchen displayBuiltTickets on any screen, coloured by how long they have waited.
  • 10Kitchen inventoryBuiltStock deducted with every sale.
  • 11Reservations and floor planBuiltTables booked from the plan the restaurant draws; seating a party opens its bill.
  • 12Buffet managementBuiltPrep planned by headcount, refills by line.

Guests4 modules

  • 13Desk check-inBuiltID checked at the desk, then the stay handed to the guest’s phone.
  • 14Guest messagingBuiltWhatsApp, SMS and email in one inbox.
  • 15Guest profilesBuiltPreferences and spend across stays and meals.
  • 16Smart locksBuiltRoom keys in Apple and Google Wallet that follow the stay dates.

Property and staff3 modules

  • 17Housekeeping and suppliesBuiltCleaning jobs and amenities, room by room.
  • 18Staff and timeclockBuiltRotas, and clock-ins verified on site.
  • 19Hotel websiteBuiltThe hotel’s own site, booking rooms and tables straight from Spear.

Where it stands

Who it’s for
Boutique hotels, B&Bs, hostels and resorts, and the restaurants, cafés and bars inside them or on their own.
How it’s paid for
A monthly subscription per property, in three packages: Restaurant, Hotel, or both in one. Hosting, updates and support included.
